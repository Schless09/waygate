# Audio attribution

The assessment clips prepared by `scripts/prepare_clips.py` are excerpts from the
**AMI Meeting Corpus**, created by the University of Edinburgh and the AMI
consortium.

Source dataset: [Trelis/tiron-eval-meetings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Trelis/tiron-eval-meetings)

The AMI material is used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0). The preparation script trims and transcodes selected meeting audio
and rebases the accompanying utterance annotations; it does not imply endorsement
by the original creators.
